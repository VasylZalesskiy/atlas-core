# Atlas 2

Atlas 2 is a separate mobile-first evolution of Atlas.

## Core idea

**Твої можливості — частинка чиєїсь задачі.**

The primary flow:

1. A person describes or dictates a task.
2. Atlas searches relevant capabilities.
3. Atlas shows possible solutions and people who can help.
4. People can communicate directly.
5. The task can be marked as solved.

## Main sections

- Задачі
- Можливості
- Потреби
- Мрії
- Я

## Product principles

- Mobile first.
- Voice input is a first-class interaction.
- Minimal forms: the user speaks/writes naturally; Atlas structures the information.
- Solving a task is the primary goal.
- Atlas 1 remains untouched while Atlas 2 is developed on this branch.
