ATLAS — TEST VERSION

WHAT IS ATLAS?

Atlas is a system designed to connect one person's need with another person's capability.

The core idea is simple:
Your capabilities can be part of someone else's solution.

For example, one person may need a generator, repair help, transport, work, advice, or a specialist. Another person may already have the generator, vehicle, knowledge, experience, or simply be willing to help. Atlas tries to find that connection and suggest the shortest practical path to a solution.

HOW DOES IT WORK?

1. Describe what you need in simple words.
2. Atlas first checks Opportunity Passports created by other users.
3. If no suitable capability is found, Atlas may search additional available external sources, places, and services.
4. If Atlas finds another person's capability, you can send them a request through Atlas.
5. The Passport owner's contact details are revealed only after they agree.

WHAT IS AN OPPORTUNITY PASSPORT?

It is your personal list of things you can offer to other people.

You can add, for example:
— skills and things you can do;
— ways you can help;
— things you can lend;
— things you can sell;
— things you can give away;
— things you can rent out;
— equipment, transport, tools, or other resources;
— professional experience or useful connections.

You can add, edit, and remove opportunities at any time.

PRIVACY

Your contact details are not shown publicly in your Opportunity Passport. Another person sends a request through Atlas, and you decide whether to accept it. Your contact is revealed only after your approval.

WHY IS THIS A TEST VERSION?

Atlas is currently being tested with real users. Some features may still work imperfectly, search results may be incomplete, and the interface will continue to change based on feedback.

We want to learn:
— whether Atlas finds useful solutions;
— whether creating an Opportunity Passport is easy;
— whether contacting another person is clear;
— which capabilities or features are still missing.

HOW CAN YOU HELP?

Try Atlas with a real need.
Create your Opportunity Passport and add several real capabilities.
If something is unclear, does not work, or you see a better way to do it, leave feedback on the home page.

Atlas is not meant to be just another listings directory. Its goal is to help people reach real solutions faster by using the capabilities of people around them.

Thank you for helping us test Atlas.
